# Market-data and resources pages (November 6, 2026)

## market-data.html — figure sources (all figures seen in public page text)

- International Energy Agency, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2024, Executive Summary (May 2024): combined market value of key energy transition minerals (copper, lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite, rare earths) about $325 billion, after a 10% contraction in 2023; projected to reach $770 billion by 2040 in the Net Zero Scenario; lithium spot prices down 75% in 2023; cobalt, nickel, and graphite down 30–45%; lithium demand up 30% in 2023; nickel, cobalt, graphite, and rare earths up 8–15%. https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2024/executive-summary
- International Energy Agency, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025, Executive Summary (May 2025): lithium demand up nearly 30% in 2024; nickel, cobalt, graphite, and rare earths up 6–8% in 2024; investment up 5% in 2024 (real growth just 2%) versus 14% in 2023; top-three mining-country share up from 73% in 2020 to 77% in 2024. https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary
- International Energy Agency, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025, Outlook for Key Minerals: lithium grows fivefold to 2040 in STEPS; graphite and nickel double; cobalt and rare earths up 50–60%; copper up 30%; about $500 billion mining capital investment required to 2040 in STEPS (about $600 billion in APS); copper about 30% and lithium about 40% supply shortfall by 2035 in STEPS. https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/overview-of-outlook-for-key-minerals
- International Energy Agency, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 (July 2026): public-finance commitments in advanced economies about $65 billion in 2025 versus about $15 billion in 2023; overall critical-minerals investment down 9% in 2025; exploration spending down more than 10%; China export controls on seven heavy rare earths in April 2025 forced some automakers to reduce utilization or halt operations — as reported by Industrial Info Resources. https://www.industrialinfo.com/news/article/iea-global-critical-minerals-risks-became-realities-in-2025--360418
- U.S. Geological Survey, 2025 List of Critical Minerals (final list published November 6, 2025; Federal Register November 7, 2025): 60 critical minerals; 50 retained from the 2022 list; 10 new (boron, copper, lead, metallurgical coal, phosphate, potash, rhenium, silicon, silver, uranium); 15 rare earth elements. https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/2025-list-critical-minerals
- U.S. Geological Survey, draft 2025 list announcement (August 2025): draft list of 54 commodities; more than 1,200 trade-disruption scenarios modeled across 84 mineral commodities and 402 industries; top-10 disruption-risk minerals samarium, rhodium, lutetium, terbium, dysprosium, gallium, germanium, gadolinium, tungsten, niobium. https://www.usgs.gov/news/science-snippet/department-interior-releases-draft-2025-list-critical-minerals
- Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck, “Critical Update! USGS Expands Mineral List” (November 12, 2025): Section 45X advanced manufacturing production credit available through 2030 only for the 50 minerals on the 2022 list; no effort underway to amend it for the 10 new minerals. https://www.bhfs.com/insight/critical-update-usgs-expands-mineral-list/
